# Requirements Document

## Introduction

logotope のマルチエージェント討論において、各チャプターで扱うべき論点をチャプター生成時に事前に決定する。各チャプターはファシリテーターによる論点1の投げかけで開幕し、チャプター全体を通して議論の流れを優先しながらも、流れが途切れたタイミングでファシリテーターが未消化論点を順次投入することで網羅性を担保する。論点の消化状況はチェックリストとして管理し、全論点消化をチャプター終了の条件とする。

## Boundary Context

- **In scope**: チャプター生成時の論点リスト付与、チャプター開幕の論点ベース投げかけ、論点チェックリストの管理、流れ優先・論点消化の統合制御、論点消化状況によるチャプター終了制御
- **Out of scope**: ターンキャップによる強制終了の廃止、公開ページでの論点表示 UI、論点の手動編集
- **Adjacent expectations**: 既存の `chapterIssues`（general / persona）生成フローは変更しない。ハードキャップの具体的な値は設計フェーズで決定する

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: チャプターごとの論点リスト生成

**Objective:** As a debate pipeline, I want each chapter to be assigned a list of key discussion points at generation time, so that the facilitator and debate executor can work through them systematically.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. When chapters are generated by `generateChapters`, the Chapter Agent shall generate a list of 3〜5 件の論点（discussionPoints）for each chapter, derived from its `title` and `focusQuestion` as well as the already-generated `generalIssues` / `personaIssues`.
2. When chapter data is persisted to Firestore（`topics/{topicId}/sessions/0`）, the Chapter Agent shall include the `discussionPoints` array for each chapter alongside `id`, `title`, and `focusQuestion`.
3. If the AI call for discussion point generation fails, the Chapter Agent shall fall back to an empty `discussionPoints` array and proceed without blocking chapter generation.
4. The `Chapter` type in `functions/src/types/chapter.types.ts` shall include a `discussionPoints: string[]` field.

---

### Requirement 2: チャプター開幕における論点1の投げかけ

**Objective:** As a facilitator, I want to open each chapter by introducing the first discussion point, so that the debate begins with a clear agenda item rather than a generic introduction.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. When a chapter begins（chapterTurnCount() === 0）and the chapter has one or more discussion points, the Facilitator shall generate an opening turn that introduces the first discussion point（discussionPoints[0]）.
2. When a chapter begins and has no discussion points（`discussionPoints` が空配列）, the Facilitator shall generate an opening turn using the existing logic based on `title` and `focusQuestion`.
3. The Debate Orchestrator shall mark discussion point 1 as "introduced" in the chapter's discussion point checklist at chapter open.

---

### Requirement 3: 論点チェックリストの管理

**Objective:** As a debate orchestrator, I want to maintain a checklist of discussion point coverage throughout the entire chapter, so that both first-phase and second-phase progress is recorded.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. The Debate Orchestrator shall initialize a discussion point checklist at chapter start, with all discussion points in "unaddressed" state.
2. The discussion point checklist shall be held in `DebateState` （実行中のメモリ）and is not required to be persisted to Firestore.
3. When evaluating whether a discussion point has been addressed, the Debate Orchestrator shall use AI evaluation against all chapter turns belonging to the current chapter（`turn.chapterId === chapter.id`）.
4. The evaluation shall treat a discussion point as addressed if the chapter turns collectively contain substantive content on that point, and as unaddressed if it has not been meaningfully raised or discussed.
5. If the discussion point evaluation fails, the Debate Orchestrator shall retain the current checklist state and fall back to the existing early-end logic to prevent blocking the debate.

---

### Requirement 4: 介入時の未消化論点投入

**Objective:** As a facilitator, I want to introduce the next unaddressed discussion point when I intervene, so that agenda coverage progresses at natural intervention moments without changing when interventions occur.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. The existing intervention trigger conditions shall remain unchanged.
2. When the Facilitator intervenes, the Debate Orchestrator shall pass the list of unaddressed discussion points to the Facilitator as context.
3. When the Facilitator intervenes and unaddressed discussion points exist, the Facilitator shall prioritize pursuing the conversation flow（deeper exploration, follow-up questions, etc.）; the Facilitator shall introduce an unaddressed discussion point only when the conversation flow does not provide a compelling direction.
4. When the Facilitator intervenes and no unaddressed discussion points remain, the Facilitator shall apply the existing intervention logic unchanged.
5. The Facilitator shall not raise multiple unaddressed discussion points in a single intervention.
6. When a discussion point is evaluated as addressed, the Debate Orchestrator shall update the checklist accordingly.

---

### Requirement 5: 論点消化状況によるチャプター終了制御

**Objective:** As a debate orchestrator, I want to control chapter termination based on discussion point coverage, so that a chapter does not end while agenda items remain unaddressed.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. While unaddressed discussion points remain in the checklist, the Debate Orchestrator shall not apply the existing early-end conditions and shall continue the chapter.
2. When all discussion points are evaluated as addressed, the Debate Orchestrator shall apply the existing early-end conditions to determine whether the chapter ends.
3. The Debate Orchestrator shall apply a hard turn cap as an absolute upper limit; this cap shall be set high enough to accommodate full discussion point coverage across chapters.
4. While the hard turn cap has been reached, the Debate Orchestrator shall end the chapter regardless of checklist state.
5. If the chapter has no discussion points（`discussionPoints` が空配列）, the Debate Orchestrator shall apply the existing early-end logic throughout without modification.
